# Overview

Start from the overview in your proposal, updated with our feedback, and
keep it current as the project changes.

## One sentence

What it is, for whom.

## Who uses it

The kinds of people, in plain terms. These become your roles.

## Why it's useful

The problem it solves, or what it makes easier.

## Key flows

Three to five things people do with it.

- A *role* can … so that …

## What's live

Your real-time feature: what happens, and who sees it happen.

## Not this semester

Ideas you're deliberately leaving out.
